How it works

Each squiggle is the line a finger draws when it swipe-types a word on a phone keyboard. Three to read every day (4, 6 and 8 letters, four guesses each), then one word to draw from memory. Solving earns ink; ink buys hints and extra guesses.

Common questions

My streak reset. A day counts when all four rounds are finished, read and draw included. The streak needs consecutive days.

I ran out of ink. Only the daily game pays ink; practice and challenges don't. Longer words and fewer guesses pay more, finishing the day adds a bonus, and hard mode pays 1.5×.

Can I play in another language? Settings → Language: English, Español and Português, each with its own keyboard, word bank and daily puzzles.

Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. There are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, erases it.

A friend's link didn't open the app. Links open the web version in the browser; it's the same game. Add it to your home screen from the Share menu and it runs offline too.

Something looks wrong. Email a screenshot and the day number (it's on the home screen) and it gets fixed.
