Squiggle privacy

Last updated October 2026

Squiggle does not collect, store or transmit personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Your puzzle progress, streak, ink balance and statistics are saved only on your device. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, erases them.

"Send a squiggle" links contain only a lightly scrambled copy of the word you typed. Sharing a result or a link uses your device's own share sheet, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send it.

The web version loads two typefaces from Google Fonts, which involves a request to Google's servers for the font files. The iPhone app works offline.
